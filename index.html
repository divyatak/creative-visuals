Creative Visuals

Sketches you play with your body. Give the camera your hands and move — each one answers to a different gesture.

Ready to play 5

  • Cloth A sheet of fabric hangs from four fingertips. Bring your hands together and it gathers into folds; pull them apart and it draws taut; move fast and it swings after you. Camera · both hands
  • Portal Hold a window open between your hands — thumbs and index fingers are its four corners. Pinch and it seals, spread and it opens, and a flock of fireflies inside talks to itself in light. Camera · both hands
  • Bioluminescent Flow Sweep a hand through dark water. The algae light where you disturb them and go on drifting once you have passed. Camera · one hand
  • Webcam Magic Drag one fingertip across a mirror made of pixels — ripple it, erase it, paint it warm. Hold up a peace sign to change what your finger does. Camera · one finger
  • (un)seen Lenticular Move across the picture and it slides between the sketch and the photograph, the way a lenticular print turns as you walk past it. Mouse or trackpad

In the workshop not playable yet

  • (e)motion Puppet a video with one hand. Gather your fingers to a bud and open them like petals — the flower opens exactly as far as you spread.
  • Audio Reactive Drop in a track and twenty thousand particles breathe with it — bass expands, mids spin, highs shimmer.
  • Particle Face Mask A sheet of particles you can press into, like a finger in sand. Then let your face do the pressing.
